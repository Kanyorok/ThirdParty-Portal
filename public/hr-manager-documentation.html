Module 2: Payroll Management

Purpose: Automated monthly salary processing with statutory compliance, eliminating manual calculations and errors.

What You Can Do:

  • ✓ Process monthly payroll with automatic calculation of:
    • Basic salary
    • Allowances (housing, transport, medical, etc.)
    • NSSF deductions (employer and employee portions)
    • NHIF deductions based on salary brackets
    • PAYE (income tax) using KRA tax bands
    • Staff loans and advances
    • Other deductions
    • Net pay calculation
  • ✓ Generate bank payment file - One click creates the file to upload to your bank for direct salary payments to all employees
  • ✓ Print individual payslips for each employee showing detailed breakdown of earnings and deductions
  • ✓ Generate P9 tax forms automatically for annual tax returns
  • ✓ View payroll reports by:
    • Company summary (total payroll costs)
    • Branch breakdown
    • Department breakdown
    • Master register (all employees)
  • ✓ Generate statutory returns for NSSF, NHIF, and KRA submissions
  • ✓ Process one-time allowances or deductions for specific employees
  • ✓ Manage staff loans with automatic monthly deductions until loan is paid
  • ✓ Track salary history and adjustments over time
  • ✓ Approve payroll before processing (requires manager approval before paying)

How the Process Works:

Step 1: HR opens a new payroll cycle for the month (e.g., "January 2026 Payroll")
Step 2: System automatically pulls employee salaries, allowances, and recurring deductions
Step 3: System calculates NSSF, NHIF, and PAYE based on current rates
Step 4: HR reviews the payroll and makes any necessary adjustments
Step 5: HR submits payroll for manager approval
Step 6: Manager reviews and approves the payroll
Step 7: System generates bank file and payslips
Step 8: Bank file is uploaded to bank portal for payment
Step 9: Payslips are distributed to employees (printed or emailed)
Step 10: Payroll cycle is closed, data is sent to finance for accounting

Business Benefits:

Accuracy

Eliminates calculation errors. Statutory deductions are always correct based on latest government rates.

Speed

What used to take 5 days now takes 1 day. Payroll processing is 80% faster.

Compliance

Automatic generation of all required statutory returns (NSSF, NHIF, KRA). No risk of missed filings.

Cost Savings

No need for external payroll bureau. Everything is done in-house with full control.

Step 4: Employee enters reason and submits request
Step 5: Supervisor receives email notification

Step 8: HR does final approval
Step 9: Employee receives email notification of approval
Step 10: Leave balance is automatically deducted

How Annual Appraisal Works:

January: HR creates new appraisal period "2026 Annual Review"
February: Employees set their goals for the year and submit for approval

Throughout Year: Employees work towards achieving their goals
December: Employees do self-assessment, rating themselves on each goal
December: Supervisors rate employees independently
January (Next Year): Performance meetings held to discuss ratings

February: Promotions and salary adjustments based on performance

Module 10: Exit Management

Purpose: Organized process for employee departures ensuring smooth handover and final settlement.

What You Can Do:

  • ✓ Record resignation when employee submits resignation letter
  • ✓ Track notice period (e.g., 1 month or 3 months as per contract)
  • ✓ Process terminations for other exit types (retirement, end of contract, death)
  • ✓ Create clearance checklist for different departments:
    • IT - return laptop, access cards
    • Finance - settle advances, loans
    • Admin - return office keys, furniture
    • HR - return policy documents
  • ✓ Track clearance completion - Each department signs off when cleared
  • ✓ Calculate final settlement automatically:
    • Salary for days worked
    • Unused leave days (paid out)
    • Gratuity/Service pay
    • Notice pay (if applicable)
    • Less: Outstanding loans
    • Less: Advances not cleared
    • Net amount payable
  • ✓ Conduct exit interview to understand why employee is leaving
  • ✓ Generate exit reports:
    • Turnover rate
    • Common reasons for leaving
    • Departments with high exits
  • ✓ Issue clearance certificate after full clearance

Exit Process Flow:

Day 1: Employee submits resignation letter
Day 2: HR registers resignation in system, notes last working day
Days 3-30: Employee serves notice period, trains replacement
Day 25: Clearance process begins - checklist sent to all departments
Days 26-30: Employee returns all company property
Day 30: Exit interview conducted
Day 31: HR calculates final settlement amount
Day 32: Finance processes final payment
Day 33: Clearance certificate issued
Day 34: Employee record status changed to "Exited"

Business Benefits:

Accurate Settlement
