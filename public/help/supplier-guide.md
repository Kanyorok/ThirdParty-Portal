## Getting Started as a Supplier

### Accessing Supplier Features
All supplier features are accessible when you activate your Supplier profile:
1. Click on your avatar in the top right corner
2. Select "Supplier" from the profile dropdown menu
3. The sidebar updates to show supplier-specific sections

### Supplier Navigation Sections
When in Supplier mode, you have access to:
- **Dashboard**: Your personalized supplier overview
- **Prequalification**: Browse and apply for prequalification categories
- **My Applications**: Track your tenders, prequalification, and RFQ submissions
- **Find RFQs**: Browse and respond to request for quotations
- **Find Tenders**: Explore available tender opportunities
- **All Documents**: Manage your documents across all applications
-  **Settings**: Update your profile and preferences

### First Steps
1. **Complete Your Profile**
   - Navigate to Settings > Profile
   - Fill in company information and registration details
   - Upload company logo
   - Provide contact information
   - Add bank details for payment processing

2. **Begin Prequalification** (if required)
   - Navigate to Prequalification section
   - Review available categories
   - Submit necessary documentation

3. **Browse Opportunities**
   - Explore available tenders
   - Review open RFQs
   - Set up notification preferences for new opportunities

## Prequalification

### What is Prequalification?
Prequalification is the process of verifying your company's capabilities and compliance in specific categories. Being prequalified may be required to bid on certain tenders or respond to RFQs.

### Accessing Prequalification
Navigate to **Prequalification** from the supplier sidebar menu.

### Prequalification Rounds
Prequalification is organized into "rounds" or categories. Each round represents:
- A specific prequalification category or sector
- A time period with start and end dates
- Specific requirements and documentation needs
- An evaluation process

### Viewing Available Rounds
The Prequalification page displays:
- **Open Rounds**: Currently accepting applications
- **Closed Rounds**: No longer accepting new applications
- **Round Status**: Start date, end date, and current state
- **Search and Filter**: Find specific categories using the search bar
- **Sorting Options**: Sort by start date or other criteria

### Starting a Prequalification Application
1. Browse the list of available prequalification rounds
2. Find a category relevant to your business
3. Click on the round to view full details
4. Review requirements and deadlines
5. Click "Apply" to start your application

### Application Process

#### Required Information
Your prequalification application typically requires:
- Company registration and legal documents
- Financial information and statements
- Tax compliance certificates
- Insurance certificates
- Quality certifications (ISO, industry-specific)
- References and past performance records
- Key personnel information
- Equipment and facility details

#### Submitting Documents
1. Upload all required documents
2. Ensure files are in accepted formats (PDF, DOC, DOCX, JPG, PNG)
3. File size limit: 10MB per document
4. Add expiry dates for time-limited certificates
5. Provide document descriptions where required

#### Application Status
Track your prequalification applications in:
- **My Applications > Prequalification**
- View all submitted prequalification applications
- Monitor evaluation progress
- Check approval status
- View scores (when available)

### Prequalification Statuses
Your applications may have these statuses:
- **Draft**: Application in progress, not yet submitted
- **Submitted**: Application received and awaiting review
- **Under Evaluation**: Being scored and reviewed
- **Additional Info Required**: Evaluators need more documentation
- **Approved**: Successfully prequalified
- **Rejected**: Did not meet requirements
- **Expired**: Prequalification validity period ended

### After Approval
Once approved:
- You'll receive notification of your approval
- Prequalification certificate may be available for download
- Validity period will be specified
- You can now bid on tenders requiring that prequalification
- Monitor expiry dates and renew before expiration

### Maintaining Prequalification
- Keep your certificates current and valid
- Upload renewed documents before expiry
- Update company information if it changes
- Respond promptly to any queries or requests for additional information
- Monitor notifications for renewal reminders

### Tenders Application Tracking

#### What You'll See

- Application status for each tender
- Key dates and deadlines
- Search and filter options
- Quick access to tender details

Your tender applications may show:
- **Draft**: Started but not yet submitted
- **Submitted**: Successfully submitted to the system
- **Under Evaluation**: Being reviewed and scored
- **Shortlisted**: Advanced to final evaluation stage
- **Awarded**: You won the tender
- **Not Successful**: Bid was not selected

#### Viewing Tender Details
Click on any tender to view:
- Tender specifications and requirements
- Your submitted bid information
- Submission timestamp
- Current status
- Any communications or clarifications

### Prequalification Application Tracking

Access via **My Applications > Prequalification**

#### What You'll See
- All your submitted prequalification applications
- Application status and progress
- Evaluation scores (when available)
- Approval certificates and validity periods
- Renewal reminders for expiring qualifications

#### Managing Prequalification Applications
- View detailed application status
- Upload additional documents if requested
- Download approval certificates

- Renew expiring prequalifications

### RFQ Application Tracking

Access via **My Applications > RFQs**  

#### What You'll See
- List of all RFQs you've responded to
- Response status for each RFQ
- Submission details and timestamps
- Award or selection outcomes

#### RFQ Response Statuses
Your RFQ responses may show:
- **Draft**: Quote in progress, not submitted
- **Submitted**: Quote successfully submitted

Click on any RFQ to view:
- RFQ specifications and quantities

All My Applications sections include:
- **Search Bar**: Find applications by keywords, reference numbers, or titles
- **Status Filters**: Filter by application status (all, open, closed, etc.)

### Notifications and Updates

## Finding and Responding to Tenders

### Accessing Tenders
Navigate to **Find Tenders** from the supplier sidebar menu.

### Browsing Available Tenders

#### Tender Listing Page
The tenders page organizes opportunities into sections:
- **Priority/Closing Soon**: Tenders with approaching deadlines
- **Active Tenders**: Currently open for bidding
- **Closed Tenders**: Past deadline, no longer accepting bids
- **Archived Tenders**: Historical tenders for reference

#### Tender Information
Each tender listing displays:
- **Title and Reference Number**: Tender identification
- **Description**: Brief overview of requirements
- **Category**: Type of goods, services, or works
- **Status**: Open, Closing Soon, or Closed
- **Deadline**: Submission deadline with countdown
- **Access Type**: Open to all or Direct invite only
- **Estimated Value**: Budget range (if provided)

#### Using Search and Filters
Find relevant tenders using:
- **Search Bar**: Search by keywords, reference number, or description
- **Status Filter**: View all tenders or filter by specific status
- **Lifecycle Filter**: Filter by active, closed, or archived tenders
- **Access Filter**: Filter by open tenders or direct invites

### Understanding Tender Status

#### Status Indicators
- **Active**: Currently open for submissions
- **Closing Soon**: Deadline within 48 hours (highlighted in red/amber)
- **Closed**: Past deadline,  no longer accepting bids
- **Archived**: Historical record
- **Opening**: Tender is being prepared to open

#### Deadline Countdown
- Shows days or hours remaining until deadline
- Color-coded for urgency:
  - Green: 5+ days remaining
  - Amber: 2-5 days remaining
  - Red: Less than 48 hours or closing soon

### Viewing Tender Details
Click on any tender to view complete information:
- Full tender description and specifications
- Detailed requirements
- Submission deadline
- Tender documents (downloadable)
- Terms and conditions
- Evaluation criteria
- Contact information

### Downloading Tender Documents
1. Open the tender detail view
2. Locate the documents section
3. Click download for each document you need
4. Documents may include tender notices, specifications, terms of reference, and bid forms

### Asking Clarification Questions
If you need clarification on tender requirements:
1. Open the tender detail page
2. Look for the clarifications or questions section
3. Submit your question through the portal
4. Questions and answers may be visible to all bidders
5. Check clarification deadlines - questions must be submitted before a specific date

### Responding to Tenders

#### Before You Start
- Verify you meet all eligibility requirements
- Check if prequalification is required for that category
- Read all tender documents thoroughly
- Note the submission deadline
- Prepare required documents and information

#### Creating Your Tender Response
1. Open the tender from the Find Tenders list
2. Review all requirements and specifications
3. Click "Submit Bid" or "Respond" button
4. Fill in the response form with required information
5. Upload necessary documents (technical proposal, pricing, certificates)
6. Review your submission for completeness
7. Submit before the deadline

#### Required Submission Components
Typical tender responses require:
- Technical proposal or methodology
- Financial proposal/pricing
- Company profile and experience
- Compliance documents and certificates
- Team information (if applicable)
- Any other documents specified in the tender

### After Submission

#### Confirming Receipt
- System confirms successful submission
- Note your bid reference number
- Download a copy of your submission for records
- Check for confirmation notification

#### Tracking Your Bid
- View your bid in My Applications > Tenders
- Monitor application status
- Check for any clarification requests
- Wait for evaluation results

### Tender Evaluation
After the deadline:
1. **Preliminary Review**: Check eligibility and completeness
2. **Technical Evaluation**: Score technical aspects
3. **Financial Evaluation**: Review pricing
4. **Final Award**: Select winning bidder(s)

Evaluation timelines vary by tender complexity and value.

### Understanding Results

#### If Awarded
- Receive award notification
- Contract documentation will be provided
- Follow procurement team instructions for next steps

#### If Not Successful
- Receive notification of  outcome
- Continue pursuing other opportunities
- Review your approach for future improvements

## Request for Quotations (RFQs)

### What is an RFQ?
A Request for Quotation (RFQ) is an invitation to submit pricing for specific goods or services with well-defined specifications. RFQs are typically for:
- Standard products or services
- Lower to medium value purchases
- Items with clear specifications
- Situations requiring quick turnaround

### Accessing RFQs
Navigate to **Find RFQs** from the supplier sidebar menu.

### Browsing RFQ Invitations

#### RFQ Listing Page
The RFQs page organizes invitations into sections:
- **Priority/Closing Soon**: RFQs with approaching deadlines
- **Open RFQs**: Currently accepting quotations
- **Submitted**: RFQs you've already responded to
- **Closed RFQs**: Past deadline or awarded

#### RFQ Information
Each RFQ listing displays:
- **Title and Description**: What's being requested
- **Reference Number**: RFQ identifier
- **Submission Deadline**: When quotes are due
- **Status**: Open, Closing Soon, Closed, or Awarded
- **Response Status**: Whether you've submitted a quote
- **Invitation Type**: Direct invite or open invitation

#### Understanding RFQ Status

**System Status:**
- **Published/Open**: Currently accepting quotations
- **Closing Soon**: Deadline within 24 hours
- **Closed**: Past deadline
- **Awarded**: Supplier has been selected

**Your Response Status:**
- **Pending**: You haven't submitted a quote yet
- **Draft**: Quote in progress but not submitted
- **Submitted**: Your quotation has been submitted
- **Accepted**: Your quote was selected

### Viewing RFQ Details
Click on any RFQ to view:
- **Specifications**: Detailed requirements
- **Quantities**: Amount of each item needed
- **Delivery Requirements**: Where and when delivery is needed
- **Quality Standards**: Any compliance or quality requirements
- **Terms and Conditions**: Payment and delivery terms
- **Submission Deadline**: Countdown timer to deadline

### Submitting Your Quotation

#### Before You Start
- Review all specifications carefully
- Verify you can meet the requirements
- Check delivery timeline feasibility
- Ensure you can provide items at required quality
- Confirm stock availability or sourcing capability

#### Creating Your Quote

1. **Open the RFQ**
   - Click on the RFQ from the Find RFQs list
   - Review all requirements

2. **Start Your Quotation**
   - Click "Submit Quote" or "Respond" button
   - Quotation form opens

3. **Enter Pricing**
   - Provide unit price for each item
   - System may auto-calculate totals
   - Include any applicable discounts
   - Add delivery/shipping costs if required

4. **Specify Terms**
   - **Quote Validity Period**: How long your pricing is valid (e.g., 30-90 days)
   - **Delivery Lead Time**: Number of days/weeks to deliver
   - **Payment Terms**: Your payment conditions
   - **Warranty Period**: Warranty offered (if applicable)

5. **Upload Supporting Documents**
   - Product datasheets or specifications
   - Compliance certificates
   - Product images (if requested)
   - Warranty documentation
   - Any other required certificates

6. **Add Additional Information**
   - Delivery notes
   - Special conditions
   - Alternative products (if permitted)
   - Bulk discounts available

7. **Review and Submit**
   - Check all pricing for accuracy
   - Verify delivery terms are feasible
   - Ensure all required fields are complete
   - Click "Submit Quotation"
   - Receive submission confirmation

### After Submission

#### Tracking Your Quote
- View in My Applications > RFQs
- Monitor response status
- Check for buyer clarifications or questions
- Wait for evaluation results

#### Responding to Clarifications
If the buyer requests more information:
1. Check your notifications
2. Go to the specific RFQ
3. Review the clarification request
4. Provide requested information or documents
5. Submit response promptly

### RFQ Evaluation
Quotes are typically evaluated on:
- **Price Competitiveness**: Primary factor
- **Delivery Timeline**: How quickly you can deliver
- **Quality and Compliance**: Meeting specifications
- **Warranty and Support**: After-sales service
- **Payment Terms**: Flexibility in payment

### Quote Outcomes

#### If Your Quote is Accepted
1. Receive acceptance notification
2. Purchase order (PO) will be issued
3. Confirm order receipt
4. Prepare items for delivery
5. Deliver according to agreed timeline
6. Provide delivery documentation
7. Submit invoice for payment

#### If Not Successful
- Receive notification that another supplier was selected
- Continue bidding on other RFQs
- Review pricing strategy for competitiveness

### Best Practices for RFQs

- **Respond Quickly**: Early submissions demonstrate reliability
- **Be Competitive**: Research market prices before quoting
- **Be Realistic**: Don't over-promise on delivery timelines
- **Quality Matters**: Never compromise on meeting specifications
- **Clear Communication**: Be precise in your quotations
- **Professional Documentation**: Submit well-presented quotes
- **Follow Up**: Monitor status regularly
- **Build Relationships**: Consistent good performance leads to more invitations

## Document Management

### Accessing Documents
Navigate to **All Documents** from the supplier sidebar menu.

### How Documents are Organized
Documents are automatically organized by source:
- **Profile**: Documents from your company profile
- **RFQ**: Documents related to RFQ invitations
- **RFQ Response**: Documents you submitted with RFQ quotes
- **Tender**: Documents from tender opportunities
- **Tender Response**: Documents you submitted with tender bids
- **Prequalification**: Documents from prequalification applications

Each source category is displayed in its own collapsible section for easy navigation.

### Viewing Documents

#### Document Information
For each document, you can view:
- **Document Name**: File name
- **File Type**: Document format (PDF, DOC, JPG, etc.)
- **Source**: Which system or application it came from
- **Upload Date**: When it was added
- **File Size**: Document size

#### Browsing Documents
1. Open the All Documents page
2. Expand any source category by clicking on it
3. View all documents within that category
4. Use search to find specific documents
5. Filter or sort as needed

### Downloading Documents
- Click on any document to download it
- Documents open in a new tab or download directly depending on file type

### Uploading Documents

- **Profile documents**: Upload via Settings > Profile

#### Tender and RFQ Documents
Submission documents:
- Technical proposals
- Financial proposals and pricing

### Document Expiry and Renewal
Some documents have expiry dates:
- The system tracks documents with expiry dates
- You'll receive alerts before documents expire
- Upload renewed versions to maintain compliance
- Keep certificates current for active prequalifications

### Best Practices

- **Keep Documents Current**: Upload renewed versions before expiration
- **Organize Properly**: Ensure documents are uploaded to correct applications
- **Use Clear Names**: Name files descriptively for easy identification

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General Questions

**Q: Do I need prequalification to bid on tenders?**
A: Not all tenders require prequalification, but many do. Check individual tender requirements to see if prequalification in a specific category is mandatory.

**Q: How long does prequalification approval take?**
A: Processing times vary depending on the category complexity and your submission completeness. Typically, it can take 2-4 weeks. Submit complete applications early to avoid delays.

**Q: Can I apply for multiple prequalification categories?**
A: Yes, you can apply for as many categories as your business qualifies for. Each category is evaluated independently.

**Q: How do I know when new tenders or RFQs are published?**
A: Enable notifications in your Settings > Notifications. You can also check the Find Tenders and Find RFQs sections regularly for new opportunities.

**Q: Can I save my application and continue later?**
A: Yes, most forms support draft saving. Use the "Save Draft" button to save your progress and return to complete it before the deadline.

**Q: What if I miss a deadline for a tender or RFQ?**
A: Unfortunately, late submissions are typically not accepted. The system enforces deadlines strictly. Always submit well before the deadline to account for any technical issues.

### Technical Questions

**Q: Which browsers are supported?**
A: The portal works best with the latest versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Microsoft Edge. Ensure your browser is up to date.

**Q: My document upload is failing. What should I do?**
A: Check that:
- File size is under 10MB
- File format is supported (PDF, DOC, DOCX, JPG, PNG, XLSX)
- Your internet connection is stable
- Try a different browser if problems persist
- Contact support if the issue continues

**Q: Where can I view my submitted bids and quotes?**
A: Navigate to My Applications from the supplier sidebar. It has three sections: Tenders, Prequalification, and RFQs, where you can track all your submissions.

**Q: How do I download documents from a tender or RFQ?**
A: Open the tender or RFQ detail page and look for the documents section. Click the download button next to each document you need.

### Process Questions

**Q: When will I know the results of my tender bid?**
A: Evaluation timelines vary by tender. Check the tender details for specific timelines. Typically, results are communicated 30-90 days after the tender closing date.

**Q: Can I withdraw my bid after submission?**
A: Contact support through My Tickets to request bid withdrawal. Withdrawals may or may not be permitted depending on the tender rules and evaluation stage.

**Q: What happens after my RFQ quote is accepted?**
A: You'll receive notification of acceptance and a purchase order (PO). Follow the delivery terms you specified in your quote and submit your invoice after delivery.

**Q: Can I partner with other suppliers for a tender?**
A: If joint ventures or consortiums are permitted in the tender documents, yes. Review the tender terms to see if partnerships are allowed.

**Q: How do I ask questions about a tender?**
A: Open the tender detail page and look for the clarifications or questions section. Submit your question through the portal before the clarification deadline specified in the tender.

### Account Questions

**Q: I forgot my password. How do I reset it?**
A: Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page. Follow the instructions sent to your registered email to reset your password.

**Q: How do I update my company information?**
A: Go to Settings > Profile to update your company details, contact information, and uploaded documents.

**Q: How do I switch to Supplier mode?**
A: Click on your avatar in the top right corner and select "Supplier" from the profile dropdown menu. The sidebar will update to show supplier features.

**Q: Can multiple users from my company access the portal?**
A: Contact support via My Tickets to inquire about setting up additional user accounts for your organization.
