## Navigation

### Sidebar Menu
The sidebar menu is your primary navigation tool featuring:
- **Collapsible Design**: Click the menu icon to expand or collapse the sidebar
- **Role-Based Navigation**: Automatically displays sections relevant to your active profile (Supplier, Tenant, or Customer)
- **Organized Sections**:
  - **Overview**: Dashboard and home
  - **Role-Specific**: Features unique to your profile type
  - **Account & Help**: Settings, Help Center, and support tickets

### Main Navigation Sections

The portal organizes features into logical sections:
- **Overview**: Dashboard with personalized metrics and quick actions
- **Role-Specific Features**: Sections unique to your business profile (Supplier, Tenant, or Customer)
- **Account & Help**: Settings, Help Center, and My Tickets

*For detailed information about features specific to your business role, refer to the role-specific guides (Supplier Guide, Tenant Guide, or Customer Guide).*

### Quick Search
Use the global search feature to quickly find pages and content:
- Click the search button in the header navigation
- Search for pages, tickets, tenders, or actions
- View recent searches and frequently visited pages
- Navigate directly to results by clicking on search results

## Dashboard

### Dashboard Overview
Your dashboard is personalized based on your active profile and displays:
- **Summary Statistics**: Key metrics relevant to your profile type
- **Recent Activity**: Latest updates and actions
- **Quick Actions**: Easy access to common tasks
- **Priority Actions**: Important items requiring your attention

The specific information displayed depends on your active business profile (Supplier, Tenant, or Customer). Each profile shows relevant metrics and actions for that particular business role.

### Switching Between Profiles
If your account has multiple business roles:
1. Click on your avatar in the top right corner
2. Select your desired profile from the dropdown menu
3. Dashboard and sidebar menu update automatically to show relevant features

## Profile & Settings

### Accessing Settings
1. Click on your avatar in the top right corner
2. Select "Account settings" from the dropdown menu
3. Or use the sidebar navigation to go to Settings

#### Profile Settings

- **Personal Information**: Name, email, phone number, address
- **Business/Company Information**: Company name, registration details, business address
- **Profile Completion Tracker**: Monitor your profile completeness percentage
- **Approval Status**: View your account approval status

*For detailed information about required fields and documentation specific to your business role (Supplier, Tenant, or Customer), refer to the corresponding role-specific guide.*

### Account Security

#### Change Password
Update your password from the Profile Settings page:
1. Navigate to Settings > Profile
2. Scroll to "Change Password" section
3. Enter your current password
4. Enter and confirm your new password
5. Click "Update Password"

**Password Requirements:**
- Minimum 8 characters
- Mix of uppercase and lowercase letters
- Include at least one number
- Include at least one special character

## Notifications

Access your notifications in two ways:
- Click the bell icon in the top navigation bar

### Notification Types
Notifications are delivered through multiple channels:

### Notification Categories
Notifications are organized by category based on your business activities:

*The specific categories you see depend on your active profile and business activities.*

### Notification Preferences
Customize your notification settings in Settings > Notifications:
- Enable or disable specific channels (in-app, email, SMS)

- Choose which types of updates you want to receive

## Support Tickets

### Creating a Support Ticket
1. Navigate to "My Tickets" from the sidebar or Help Center
2. Click "Create New Ticket" or the plus (+) button
3. Fill in the ticket details:
   - **Subject**: Brief, descriptive title of your issue
   - **Category/Type**: Select the appropriate category for your request
   - **Priority/Severity**: Choose the urgency level (low, medium, high, critical)
   - **Message**: Provide a detailed description of your issue or request
   - **Attachments**: Add screenshots, documents, or files to help explain your issue
4. Click "Submit" to create your ticket

### Viewing Your Tickets
Access all your support tickets in the My Tickets section:
- View list of all your submitted tickets
- See ticket status at a glance
- Quick search to find specific tickets
- Filter options:
  - **Status**: All, Open, Pending, Resolved, Closed
  - **Severity**: Filter by priority level
  - **Sort**: Newest first or Oldest first

### Ticket Information
Each ticket displays:
- Subject and unique ticket ID
- Current status
- Priority level
- Response/message count
- Creation and last update timestamps

### Ticket Statuses
Your tickets can have these statuses:
1. **Open**: Newly created, ticket has been received
2. **Pending**: Awaiting response or additional information
3. **In Progress**: Support team is actively working on your issue
4. **Resolved**: Issue has been addressed and fixed
5. **Closed**: Ticket is complete and archived

### Interacting with Tickets
- **View Details**: Click on any ticket to see the full conversation
- **Add Messages**: Reply to ongoing tickets with additional information
- **Track Responses**: See response count to monitor activity
- **Receive Updates**: Get notified when support team responds

### Best Practices for Support Tickets
- **Clear Subject Lines**: Use descriptive titles that summarize your issue
- **Detailed Descriptions**: Explain the problem thoroughly:
  - What you were trying to do
  - What happened instead
  - Any error messages you received
- **Include Screenshots**: Visual aids help support understand your issue faster
- **One Issue Per Ticket**: Create separate tickets for different problems
- **Include Context**: Mention:
  - When the issue started
  - Which page or feature is affected
  - Your browser type and version (if relevant)
- **Respond Promptly**: Reply quickly when support requests more information
- **Be Professional**: Maintain courteous communication with support staff

## Common Tasks

### Using Quick Search
The quick search feature helps you navigate the portal efficiently:
1. Click the search button in the header navigation
2. Type keywords for pages, features, or documents
3. Click on search results to navigate to them
4. Recent searches and frequently accessed pages appear for quick access

### Uploading Documents
1. Navigate to the relevant document upload section
2. Click "Upload" button or drag-and-drop files into the upload area
3. Ensure files meet requirements:

### Downloading Documents
- Click the download icon/button next to any document

- Some documents may open in a new tab before downloading

- **Search**: Text search within the current list
- **Status Filters**: Filter by status (Active, Pending, Completed, etc.)
- **Date Filters**: Select date ranges or specific periods
- **Sorting**: Click column headers to sort ascending/descending
- **Clear Filters**: Reset all filters to see complete list

### Working with Tables
Browse and manage data in tables:
- Click on rows to view details
- Use pagination controls to navigate multiple pages
- Adjust items per page when available
- Use action buttons or menus for row-specific operations

### Form Validation and Submission
When filling out forms:
- **Required Fields**: Marked with asterisks (*) - must be completed

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Can't Log In**
- Verify your email address and password are correct
- Check that Caps Lock is off
- Try the "Forgot Password" link to reset your password
- Clear your browser cache and cookies
- Try using a different browser
- Contact support if the issue persists

**Page Not Loading or Displaying Incorrectly**
- Check your internet connection
- Refresh the page using F5 or Ctrl/Cmd + R
- Clear your browser cache and cookies
- Try opening the page in incognito/private browsing mode
- Disable browser extensions temporarily
- Update your browser to the latest version
- Try a different browser

**Document Upload Fails**
- Check file size - ensure it's under the maximum limit (typically 10MB)
- Verify the file format is supported (PDF, DOC, DOCX, JPG, PNG)
- Ensure you have a stable internet connection
- Try compressing large files before uploading
- Check that the file isn't corrupted - try opening it locally first
- Use a different browser if the problem persists

**Changes Not Saving**
- Wait for the save confirmation message
- Check for error messages or highlighted fields
- Verify all required fields (marked with *) are completed
- Ensure your internet connection is stable
- Check that your session hasn't expired - you may need to log in again
- Try refreshing the page and re-entering the information

**Search Not Working or Not Showing Results**
- Try using different keywords or search terms
- Check spelling and try variations
- Use the filter options to narrow down results
- Clear your search and try again
- Make sure you're in the correct profile/role for the content you're searching

**Notifications Not Appearing**
- Check your notification preferences in Settings > Notifications
- Verify you haven't muted notifications
- Check your email spam/junk folder for email notifications
- Refresh the notifications page
- Try logging out and back in

**Sidebar or Menu Not Responding**
- Click the menu icon to toggle the sidebar
- Refresh the page
- Clear browser cache
- Check if browser extensions are interfering
- Try a different browser

### Browser Cache Clearing Instructions

**Chrome**
1. Press `Ctrl + Shift + Delete` (Windows) or `Cmd + Shift + Delete` (Mac)
2. Select "Cached images and files"
3. Choose time range (recommend "All time" for troubleshooting)
4. Click "Clear data"

**Firefox**
1. Press `Ctrl + Shift + Delete` (Windows) or `Cmd + Shift + Delete` (Mac)
2. Select "Cache"
3. Click "Clear Now"

**Safari**
1. Open Safari menu > Preferences (or Settings)
2. Go to Privacy tab
3. Click "Manage Website Data"
4. Click "Remove All" or select specific sites
5. Confirm the action

**Microsoft Edge**
1. Press `Ctrl + Shift + Delete`
2. Select "Cached images and files"
3. Choose time range
4. Click "Clear now"

## Glossary

**Dashboard**: Your personalized home page displaying summary information, key metrics, and quick actions relevant to your active profile.

**Profile**: Your user account information, including personal details, business information, and settings. Can also refer to your business role (Supplier, Tenant, or Customer).

**Active Profile**: The currently selected business role that determines which features and sections are visible in the portal.

**Profile Type**: Your business classification - Supplier (vendor), Tenant (property renter), or Customer (insurance client).



**Notification**: An alert or message about portal activity, delivered via in-app, email, or SMS.

**Support Ticket**: A formal request for help or issue report submitted through the My Tickets system.

**Session**: The period from when you log in until you log out or your session expires due to inactivity.

**Sidebar**: The navigation menu on the left side of the portal that provides access to all major features and sections.

**Quick Search**: The search feature in the header navigation that allows rapid navigation to pages and features.

**Filter**: A tool to narrow down lists and search results based on specific criteria like status, date, or category.

**Document Upload**: The process of attaching files (PDFs, images, Word documents) to your profile, applications, or tickets.

**Profile Completion**: A percentage indicating how much of your required profile information has been filled out.

**Approval Status**: The current state of your account or application (e.g., Approved, Pending, In Review).

**Priority Actions**: Important tasks or items that require your immediate attention, displayed on your dashboard.
