## Getting Started as a Customer

### Switching to Customer Profile

If you have multiple profiles (Supplier, Tenant, or Customer), switch to Customer mode:
1. Click on your avatar in the top right corner
2. Select **Customer** from the profile dropdown
3. The sidebar will update to show customer-specific features

### Your Customer Dashboard

Your dashboard is the central hub where you can:
- View summary information
- Access notifications
- Navigate to different customer sections via the sidebar

### Customer Navigation

The customer sidebar provides access to:
- **My Policies**: View and manage your insurance policies
- **Settings**: Update your profile and preferences
- **Help Center**: Access guides and documentation
- **My Tickets**: Create and track support tickets

### Profile Setup

**Complete Your Profile:**
1. Navigate to Settings > Profile
2. Update your personal information
3. Add contact details
4. Upload a profile picture (optional)
5. Save your changes

**Note:** Maintaining accurate profile information ensures smooth service delivery and communication.

## Managing Your Profile

### Accessing Profile Settings

Navigate to **Settings > Profile** from the sidebar.

### Profile Information

Update and manage your profile information:

**Personal Details:**
- Full name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Profile picture

**Contact Information:**
- Primary contact details
- Alternative contact methods
- Communication preferences

**Account Settings:**
- Password management
- Notification preferences
- Display settings

### Updating Your Information

1. Go to Settings > Profile
2. Click on the field you want to update
3. Enter new information
4. Save changes
5. Receive confirmation

**Important:** Keep your contact information up to date to ensure you receive important notifications and communications.

### Notifications

Manage your notification preferences in Settings:
- Email notifications
- Portal notifications
- Notification frequency
- Types of alerts

Enable or disable specific notification types based on your preferences.

## My Policies

### Accessing My Policies

Navigate to **My Policies** from the customer sidebar menu.

### Policy Overview

The My Policies section is where you can view and manage your insurance policies.

**Note:** Policy features and availability depend on your specific insurance setup and services. Contact support for detailed policy information and management options.

### What You Can Do

From the My Policies section:
- View your active insurance policies
- Access policy information
- Check coverage details
- Review policy status

### Policy Information

Typical policy information includes:
- Policy number and type
- Coverage details
- Policy period (start and end dates)
- Premium information
- Status (active, pending, expired)

### Policy Documents

Policy documents and certificates are typically provided:
- Via email from your insurance provider
- Through direct communication with your agent
- As physical copies when policies are issued

Keep all policy documents in a safe place for future reference.

### Managing Your Policies

For comprehensive policy management including:
- Detailed coverage information
- Making claims
- Premium payments
- Policy renewals
- Coverage modifications
- Beneficiary updates

**Contact your insurance provider or agent directly** for these services.

### Policy Support

For policy-related assistance:
- Contact customer support via **My Tickets**
- Reach out to your insurance agent or provider
- Use the **Help Center** for general information
- Check your email for policy communications

## Getting Support

### Support Options

Navigate to **My Tickets** from the sidebar to access support.

**Create a Support Ticket:**
1. Click "Create New Ticket" button
2. Provide ticket details:
   - Subject/Title
   - Category (if applicable)
   - Description of your issue or question
3. Attach screenshots or documents if helpful
4. Submit your ticket
5. Track ticket status and responses

**Check the Help Center:**
- Navigate to **Help Center** from the sidebar
- Browse available guides and documentation
- Search for specific topics
- Access FAQs and resources
- Download guide PDFs for offline reference

### Common Support Topics

- Technical issues with the portal
- Account and profile management
- Questions about insurance services
- General portal navigation
- Password reset and login issues
- Notification settings

### Response Times

Support tickets are typically:
- Acknowledged within 24 hours
- Resolved based on issue complexity
- Updated regularly with progress

Check your ticket status in My Tickets to see updates and responses.

### Help Center Resources

The Help Center provides:
- Guide documentation (this guide, general guide, etc.)
- FAQs and answers to common questions
- Portal navigation tips
- PDF downloads for offline reading

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General Questions

**Q: How do I switch to Customer profile?**  
A: Click on your avatar in the top right corner and select "Customer" from the profile dropdown menu.

**Q: Where can I view my insurance policies?**  
A: Navigate to **My Policies** from the customer sidebar menu to view your insurance policies.

**Q: How do I update my profile information?**  
A: Go to Settings > Profile, update the fields you need to change, and save your changes.

**Q: Can I manage multiple types of profiles (Supplier, Tenant, Customer)?**  
A: Yes, if your account has multiple profiles, you can switch between them using the avatar dropdown in the top right corner.

### Policy Questions

**Q: How do I access policy documents?**  
A: Policy documents are typically provided via email from your insurance provider or through direct communication with your agent. Contact your insurance provider for policy documents.

**Q: Can I make insurance claims through the portal?**  
A: For claims processing, contact your insurance provider or agent directly. They will guide you through the claims process.

**Q: How do I make premium payments?**  
A: Premium payments are handled through your insurance provider. Contact them for payment methods and schedules.

**Q: Where can I find my policy number?**  
A: Your policy number is available in the My Policies section and in policy documents provided by your insurance provider.

### Technical Questions

**Q: I forgot my password. How do I reset it?**  
A: Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page. Follow the instructions sent to your registered email to reset your password.

**Q: I'm not receiving notification emails. What should I do?**  
A: Check your spam/junk folder, verify your email address in Settings > Profile, and ensure notification preferences are enabled.

**Q: Can I access the portal on my mobile device?**  
A: Yes, the portal is responsive and works on mobile browsers. Access it through your phone or tablet's web browser.

**Q: How do I change my notification preferences?**  
A: Navigate to Settings and adjust your notification preferences for email and portal notifications.

### Account Questions

**Q: How do I change my email address?**  
A: Go to Settings > Profile, update your email address, and save changes.

**Q: Can I change my password?**  
A: Yes, go to Settings > Profile or use the "Forgot Password" feature on the login page to set a new password.

**Q: How do I update my contact information?**  
A: Navigate to Settings > Profile, update your phone number, address, or other contact details, and save.

**Q: Who do I contact for insurance-specific questions?**  
A: Contact your insurance agent or provider directly for policy-specific questions, claims, payments, and coverage details.

### Support Questions

**Q: How do I create a support ticket?**  
A: Navigate to My Tickets from the sidebar, click "Create New Ticket", fill in the details, and submit.

**Q: How long does it take to get a response to my ticket?**  
A: Support tickets are typically acknowledged within 24 hours. Resolution time depends on the issue complexity.

**Q: Can I track my support ticket status?**  
A: Yes, go to My Tickets to view all your tickets and their current status.

**Q: Where can I find help documentation?**  
A: Navigate to Help Center from the sidebar to access all guides, FAQs, and documentation.
