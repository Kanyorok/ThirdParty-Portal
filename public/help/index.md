## About the Portal

The Third Party Portal is a unified platform designed to streamline interactions between the organization and its external stakeholders:

- **Suppliers**: Manage vendor relationships, tenders, RFQs, and procurement processes
- **Tenants**: Handle property leasing, maintenance requests, and billing
- **Customers**: Manage insurance policies, claims, and payments

## Getting Started

### First Time Login
1. Navigate to the login page
2. Enter your credentials (email and password)
3. If you've forgotten your password, use the "Forgot Password" link
4. After successful login, you'll be directed to your personalized dashboard

### Profile Setup
- Complete your profile information in Settings
- Upload required documents based on your user type
- Set your communication preferences

### Dashboard Overview
Your dashboard provides a summary of:
- Recent activities
- Pending actions
- Quick access to key features
- Important notifications

## User Roles

### Supplier
Suppliers can:
- Apply for prequalification
- Browse and bid on tenders
- Respond to RFQs (Request for Quotations)
- Track application status
- Manage documents and contracts

### Tenant
Tenants can:
- Browse available properties
- Submit property interests
- Manage lease agreements
- Pay invoices
- Submit maintenance requests
- Track work orders

### Customer
Customers can:
- View and manage insurance policies
- Track policy status
- Access coverage documents
- Make payments

## Support

If you need additional assistance:
- **Help Tickets**: Create a support ticket from [My Tickets](/dashboard/help/tickets)
- **Email**: Contact support at support@thirdpartyportal.com
- **Hours**: Monday - Friday, 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I reset my password?
Visit the [Forgot Password](/forgot-password) page and follow the instructions sent to your email.

### Who can I contact for technical support?
Create a ticket in the [My Tickets](/dashboard/help/tickets) section or email support@thirdpartyportal.com.

### How do I update my profile information?
Go to Settings > Profile to update your personal information and preferences.

### What browsers are supported?
The portal works best on the latest versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge.
