## Getting Started as a Tenant

### Switching to Tenant Profile

If you have multiple profiles (Supplier, Tenant, or Customer), switch to Tenant mode:
1. Click on your avatar in the top right corner
2. Select **Tenant** from the profile dropdown
3. The sidebar will update to show tenant-specific features

### Your Tenant Dashboard

Your dashboard is the central hub where you can:
- View summary metrics and key information
- Access recent activity and notifications
- Navigate to different tenant sections via the sidebar

### Tenant Navigation

The tenant sidebar provides quick access to:
- **Rentable Properties**: Browse available units
- **My Interests**: Track property interest applications
- **Leases**: Manage your lease agreements
- **Invoices**: View billing and payment history
- **Maintenance**: Submit and track service requests

### Profile Setup

**Complete Your Profile:**
1. Navigate to Settings > Profile
2. Update your personal information
3. Add contact details
4. Upload a profile picture (optional)
5. Save your changes

**Note:** Profile completion helps property managers process your applications faster.

## Browsing Rentable Properties

### Accessing Properties
Navigate to **Rentable Properties** from the tenant sidebar.

### Property Listing View

#### Search and Filter
- **Search Bar**: Search properties by name or location
- **Location Filter**: Filter by locality/area
- **View All**: Browse all available properties in the system

#### Property Cards

Each property card displays:
- **Property Name**: Building or property name
- **Location**: Area/locality
- **Availability Badge**: Indicates vacant units or occupancy status
- **Property Statistics**:
  - Total units
  - Vacant units
  - Blocks and floors
  - Vacancy rate
- **View Details**: Button to explore property structure

### Viewing Property Details

Click **View Details** on any property card to see the full structure:

#### Property Structure

Properties are organized hierarchically:
- **Property**: The main building or complex
- **Blocks**: Separate buildings within the property
- **Floors**: Levels within each block
- **Units**: Individual rental units on each floor

#### Unit Information

For each unit, you can see:
- **Unit Name/Number**: Unit identifier
- **Floor**: Which floor the unit is on
- **Block**: Which block it belongs to
- **Availability Status**: Vacant or Occupied
- **Unit Type**: Category or classification (if specified)

#### Property Actions

- **Express Interest**: Submit an application for a specific unit
- **Print Summary**: Generate a printable property overview
- **View Structure**: Expand blocks and floors to see all units

**Note:** Property viewing schedules, photos, and detailed amenity lists are managed directly with property managers. Contact information is provided in property details.

## Submitting Property Interests

### What is a Property Interest?

A property interest is your formal application to rent a specific unit. It notifies property management of your intent to lease.

### Accessing My Interests

Navigate to **My Interests** from the tenant sidebar to:
- View all submitted interests
- Track application status
- Filter by time period (current, upcoming, past)
- Search through your applications

### Submitting an Interest

#### From Property Details Page

1. Browse **Rentable Properties**
2. Click **View Details** on a property
3. Expand blocks and floors to find your desired unit
4. Click **Express Interest** button
5. Complete the interest application form
6. Review and submit

#### Application Information

When submitting an interest, you'll provide:
- Selected unit details
- Intended start date
- Payment frequency preference (Monthly, Quarterly, Weekly, etc.)
- Additional information or special requests
- Contact details

**Note:** Required documents (ID, proof of income, references) may be requested separately by property management.

### Interest Status Tracking

Track your applications in **My Interests** with these statuses:

- **Submitted**: Application received, awaiting review
- **Under Review**: Being evaluated by property team
- **Shortlisted**: Passed initial screening
- **Approved**: Interest accepted, lease preparation in progress
- **Rejected**: Application not successful
- **Withdrawn**: You cancelled the application
- **Expired**: Application validity period ended

### Viewing Interest Details

Click on any interest to view full details:
- **Property Information**: Name, location, unit
- **Dates**: Interest period (start and end dates)
- **Payment Details**: Frequency and terms
- **Status**: Current application status
- **Submission Date**: When you applied
- **Additional Information**: Notes or special requests

### Filtering Interests

Filter your interests by time window:
- **All**: View all interests regardless of date
- **Current**: Active interests within current period
- **Upcoming**: Future-dated interests
- **Past**: Completed or expired interests

### Multiple Applications

You can:
- Apply for multiple units simultaneously
- Track all applications in one place
- Each application is evaluated independently

## Managing Leases

### Accessing Your Leases

Navigate to **Leases** from the tenant sidebar.

### Viewing Lease Information

The leases page displays all your lease agreements with key details:

#### Lease Overview

For each lease, you can see:
- **Lease Number**: Unique lease identifier
- **Property Name**: Building or property
- **Unit**: Specific unit number
- **Status**: Active, Inactive, Pending, Expired, or Terminated
- **Lease Period**: Start and end dates
- **Monthly Rent**: Rental amount and currency
- **Payment Frequency**: How often rent is due (Monthly, Weekly, etc.)

#### Lease Details Sheet

Click on any lease to view comprehensive details:

**Basic Information:**
- Lease number and property details
- Unit and block information
- Tenant name
- Lease status

**Financial Details:**
- Monthly rent amount
- Currency
- Payment frequency
- Security deposit amount

**Dates:**
- Lease start date
- Lease end date
- Creation/approval dates

**Lease Actions:**
- Renew lease
- Terminate lease
- View full lease details

### Lease Renewal

#### Initiating Renewal

1. Open the lease you want to renew
2. Click **Renew Lease** button
3. Review current lease terms
4. Select requested extension term:
   - 6 Months
   - 12 Months
   - 24 Months
5. Submit renewal request

#### What to Expect

- Your current monthly rent is displayed for reference
- Property managers will review your request
- You may be contacted regarding any rate adjustments
- Renewal subject to approval and updated verification
- Once approved, new lease terms will be communicated

**Note:** Extensions are subject to approval. Property management may adjust rent or terms based on market conditions.

### Lease Termination

#### Termination Notice

If you need to end your lease:

1. Open the lease you want to terminate
2. Click **Terminate Lease** button
3. Review termination policy notice
4. Complete the termination form:
   - **Reason for Vacating**: Select from options
     - Professional Relocation
     - Upsizing / Need more space
     - Financial Reasons
     - Other
   - **Requested Last Day**: Choose your move-out date
5. Review deposit and balance information
6. Submit termination notice

#### Notice Requirements

- **Standard Notice Period**: 60 days required
- **Earliest Penalty-Free Move-Out**: Calculated from submission date
- **Held Deposit**: Shown for reference
- **Current Balance**: Outstanding rent or charges

#### After Submission

- Property management reviews your request
- Move-out inspection will be scheduled
- Deposit refunds processed within 14 business days post-inspection
- Final settlement communicated

**Important:** Early termination without proper notice may result in penalties or deposit forfeit based on lease terms.

### Lease Status Indicators

- **Active**: Currently in effect, valid lease
- **Inactive**: Not currently active
- **Pending**: Awaiting approval or processing
- **Expired**: Lease period has ended
- **Terminated**: Lease ended early by termination

### Search and Filter

Use the search feature to:
- Find specific leases by property name
- Search by unit number
- Filter by lease status
- Locate leases quickly

## Invoices and Payments

### Accessing Invoices

Navigate to **Invoices** from the tenant sidebar.

### Invoice Listing

The invoices page displays all your billing records:

#### Invoice Information

For each invoice, you can see:
- **Invoice Number**: Unique invoice identifier
- **Invoice Date**: When the invoice was created
- **Due Date**: Payment deadline
- **Amount**: Total invoice amount with currency
- **Status**: Payment status (Paid, Pending, Overdue)
- **Property/Unit**: Associated property details

### Invoice Status Indicators

- **Paid**: Invoice has been fully settled
- **Pending**: Payment not yet received
- **Overdue**: Past the due date, payment required urgently

#### Status Badges:
- **Paid**: Green badge with checkmark icon
- **Pending**: Amber/yellow badge with clock icon
- **Overdue**: Red badge with alert icon

### Viewing Invoice Details

Click on any invoice to open the detailed view:

**Invoice Header:**
- Invoice number and date
- Due date
- Property and unit information
- Tenant details

**Charges Breakdown:**
- **Rent**: Monthly rental charge
- **Service Charge**: Building maintenance fees
- **Utilities**: Water, electricity (if included)
- **Other Charges**: Parking, late fees, etc.

**Totals:**
- **Subtotal**: Sum of all charges
- **Tax**: VAT or applicable tax (with rate %)
- **Total Amount**: Final amount due

**Payment Information:**
- Payment status
- Amount paid (if partial payment)
- Balance remaining

### Downloading Invoices

1. Click on an invoice to open details
2. Click the **Download PDF** button
3. Invoice downloads as a PDF file
4. Save for your records

**Uses for Downloaded Invoices:**
- Personal record keeping
- Expense tracking
- Tax documentation
- Proof of rent payment

### Making Payments

**Note:** Payment processing is typically handled through:
- Bank transfers to landlord account

- Direct payments to property management

Payment details and instructions are provided by property management. Contact them for:
- Payment account details
- Accepted payment methods
- Payment reference format
- Payment confirmation

### Search and Filter

Use the search feature to:
- Find specific invoices by number
- Search by property name
- Filter by status (Paid, Pending, Overdue)
- Locate invoices quickly

### Best Practices

- **Check Regularly**: Review invoices monthly
- **Pay on Time**: Avoid late fees by paying before due date

## Maintenance Requests

### When to Submit Maintenance Request

Submit requests for:
- **Plumbing**: Leaks, clogs, drainage issues
- **Electrical**: Power issues, socket problems, lighting
- **HVAC**: Heating/cooling not working
- **Appliances**: Broken fridge, stove, water heater
- **Structural**: Doors, windows, walls, ceilings
- **Pest Control**: Insects, rodents, pest issues
- **Security**: Locks, access issues
- **Other**: Any maintenance or repair needs

### Accessing Maintenance

Navigate to **Maintenance** from the tenant sidebar.

### Creating a Maintenance Request

#### Step 1: Open Request Form
Click **New Request** button to open the maintenance request sheet.

#### Step 2: Complete Request Details

**Property Selection:**
- Select the property/unit where the issue is located
- Choose from your active lease properties

**Issue Title:**
- Brief, descriptive title (e.g., "Kitchen sink blockage")
- Should clearly identify the problem

**Category:**
- Select issue type from categories:
  - Plumbing
  - Electrical
  - HVAC
  - Appliance
  - Structural
  - Pest Control
  - Security
  - Carpentry
  - Painting
  - Other

**Priority Level:**
- **Emergency**: Urgent safety or security issue
- **High**: Significant problem needing quick attention
- **Medium**: Important but not urgent (default)
- **Low**: Minor issue, can wait

**Description:**
- Detailed explanation of the problem
- Include:
  - What's happening
  - When it started
  - Location within unit
  - Any safety concerns
  - Previous occurrences

**Attachments (Optional):**
- Upload photos or videos showing the issue
- Visual evidence helps technicians prepare
- Click to upload media files

#### Step 3: Submit Request
1. Review all information
2. Click **Submit Request**
3. Receive confirmation
4. Request added to your maintenance list

### Priority Levels Explained

**Emergency:**
- No water or power
- Gas leaks
- Severe flooding
- Broken locks/security issues
- Fire safety equipment failure
- Requires immediate response

**High:**
- Major leaks
- Non-functioning essential appliances
- Heating/cooling failure
- Significant electrical issues
- Attended within 24-48 hours

**Medium (Default):**
- Minor leaks
- Non-emergency repairs
- General maintenance
- Standard service timeline

**Low:**
- Cosmetic issues
- Non-urgent improvements
- Minor wear and tear
- Scheduled at convenience

### Tracking Your Requests

View all maintenance requests in the Maintenance page:

**Request List Display:**
- **Ticket Number**: Unique reference (e.g., #12345)
- **Issue Title**: What you named the request
- **Request Date**: When you submitted it
- **Property/Unit**: Where the issue is located
- **Status**: Current request status
- **Priority**: Urgency level
- **Actions**: View details button

### Request Status

- **Open**: New request, waiting to be assigned
- **In Progress**: Technician assigned, work underway
- **Resolved**: Issue fixed, awaiting confirmation
- **Closed**: Completed and confirmed

**Status Colors:**
- Open: Purple badge
- In Progress: Blue badge
- Resolved: Green badge
- Closed: Gray badge

### After Submission

**What Happens Next:**
1. Property management receives your request
2. Request is reviewed and prioritized
3. Technician assigned (emergency requests handled immediately)
4. You may be contacted for access or clarification
5. Repair scheduled
6. Work completed
7. Status updated to Resolved/Closed

**Communication:**
- You'll receive notifications about status changes
- Property management may contact you to:
  - Confirm details
  - Schedule access time
  - Provide updates
  - Confirm completion

### Search and Filter

- Search requests by ticket number or issue title
- Filter by status (Open, In Progress, Resolved, Closed)
- Filter by priority level
- Find specific requests quickly

### Best Practices

**Reporting:**
- Report issues promptly
- Be specific in descriptions
- Add photos when possible
- Choose accurate priority level
- Don't attempt complex repairs yourself

**Communication:**
- Respond to follow-up questions quickly
- Provide access when technicians visit
- Notify property management if issue worsens
- Confirm when work is completed satisfactorily

**Preparation:**
- Clear access to problem area
- Secure pets during service visits
- Be available or arrange access
- Have questions ready for technician

### Emergency Situations

For genuine emergencies:
1. Ensure your safety first
2. Take immediate action (e.g., shut off water if flooding)
3. Submit maintenance request with **Emergency** priority
4. Call property management emergency number (if provided)
5. Document the situation with photos

**What Qualifies as Emergency:**
- Life safety issues
- Security breaches
- Major water/gas leaks
- Total power loss
- No heating in extreme cold
- Situations causing property damage

## Tips for Tenants

### Finding the Right Property

- **Browse Regularly**: Check Rentable Properties frequently for new listings
- **Use Search**: Use the search feature to find properties in specific areas
- **Filter by Location**: Use locality filters to narrow down options
- **Review Details**: Carefully expand property structures to see all available units
- **Check Vacancy**: Look for properties with high vacancy rates for better availability
- **Note Key Features**: Pay attention to blocks, floors, and unit types

### Submitting Strong Interest Applications

- **Complete Your Profile**: Ensure your profile is fully filled out before applying
- **Choose Right Unit**: Select units that match your needs and budget
- **Be Clear**: Provide detailed additional information in your application
- **Realistic Dates**: Choose reasonable intended start dates
- **Payment Frequency**: Select a payment frequency you can maintain
- **Follow Up**: Monitor your interests regularly for status updates

### Managing Your Lease

- **Read Carefully**: Understand all lease terms before signing
- **Know Your Dates**: Be aware of lease start and end dates
- **Plan for Renewal**: Start renewal process well before lease expires
- **Proper Notice**: Give required notice if terminating (usually 60 days)
- **Document Everything**: Keep copies of all lease-related documents
- **Track Renewals**: Monitor lease expiry and plan accordingly

### Staying on Top of Payments

- **Check Invoices**: Review invoices as soon as they're issued
- **Pay on Time**: Always pay before the due date to avoid late fees
- **Download Records**: Save invoice PDFs for your financial records
- **Verify Charges**: Review all charges for accuracy
- **Report Issues**: Contact property management immediately about billing errors
- **Keep Receipts**: Save all payment confirmations

### Effective Maintenance Management

- **Report Promptly**: Submit maintenance requests as soon as issues arise
- **Be Detailed**: Provide clear, specific descriptions of problems
- **Add Photos**: Include pictures to help technicians understand the issue
- **Choose Correct Priority**: Select appropriate urgency level
- **Track Status**: Monitor request status through the portal
- **Respond Quickly**: Answer any follow-up questions from property management
- **Prevent Issues**: Perform basic upkeep to avoid problems

### Communication Best Practices

- **Be Professional**: Maintain courteous communication with property management
- **Respond Promptly**: Reply to messages and requests quickly
- **Use Portal**: Submit requests through the portal for proper tracking
- **Keep Records**: Document all communications and interactions
- **Be Clear**: Communicate issues clearly and without emotion
- **Follow Channels**: Use appropriate contact methods for different issues

### Organization and Record-Keeping

- **Save Documents**: Download and organize all lease and invoice documents
- **Track Payments**: Maintain a record of all rent payments
- **Monitor Deadlines**: Keep a calendar of important dates
- **Organize Invoices**: Save invoices by date for easy reference
- **Keep Maintenance Records**: Track all maintenance requests and resolutions
- **Store Safely**: Keep digital and physical copies of important documents

### Building Good Relationships

- **Be Respectful**: Treat property, staff, and neighbors with respect
- **Follow Rules**: Adhere to building policies and lease terms
- **Communicate Issues**: Report problems early before they escalate
- **Be Considerate**: Keep noise levels reasonable, especially during quiet hours
- **Maintain Property**: Keep your unit clean and in good condition
- **Pay Reliably**: Consistently pay rent on time

### Planning Ahead

**For Lease Renewal:**
- Start review process 90+ days before expiry
- Research current market rates in your area
- Decide if you want to stay or move
- Prepare for potential rent adjustments
- Submit renewal request early

**For Moving Out:**
- Give proper notice (typically 60 days)
- Clean property thoroughly
- Document property condition
- Arrange final inspection
- Provide forwarding address
- Return all keys and access cards

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General Questions

**Q: How do I switch to Tenant profile?**
A: Click on your avatar in the top right corner and select "Tenant" from the profile dropdown menu.

**Q: How do I know which properties are available?**
A: Navigate to Rentable Properties. Properties show their vacancy status and available units. Expand the property structure to see individual unit availability.

**Q: Can I apply for multiple units?**
A: Yes, you can submit interests for multiple properties or units. Each application is tracked separately in My Interests.

**Q: How long does it take to hear about my property interest?**
A: Processing times vary. Monitor the status in My Interests. Property management will contact you when there's an update.

### Property Interest Questions

**Q: What information do I need to submit a property interest?**
A: You'll need to provide your intended start date, preferred payment frequency, and any additional information or special requests.

**Q: Can I withdraw my property interest?**
A: Yes, you can withdraw an interest application. The status will update accordingly in your My Interests section.

**Q: What do the different interest statuses mean?**
A: 
- **Submitted**: Application received
- **Under Review**: Being evaluated
- **Shortlisted**: Passed initial review
- **Approved**: Interest accepted
- **Rejected**: Not successful
- **Withdrawn**: You cancelled
- **Expired**: Application period ended

### Lease Questions

**Q: How do I renew my lease?**
A: Open your lease in the Leases section, click "Renew Lease", select your desired extension term (6, 12, or 24 months), and submit the renewal request.

**Q: How much notice do I need to give before moving out?**
A: Standard notice is 60 days. Review your specific lease terms to confirm. Use the Terminate Lease feature to submit proper notice.

**Q: Can I end my lease early?**
A: You can submit a termination request, but early termination may involve penalties depending on your lease terms. The termination form will calculate your earliest penalty-free move-out date.

**Q: Where can I view my lease details?**
A: Go to Leases from the sidebar, then click on any lease to view full details including dates, rent, status, and financial information.

### Invoice and Payment Questions

**Q: Where do I view my invoices?**
A: Navigate to Invoices from the tenant sidebar. All your billing records are listed there.

**Q: How do I download an invoice?**
A: Click on any invoice to open the details, then click the "Download PDF" button to save a copy.

**Q: What payment methods are accepted?**
A: Payment methods and instructions are provided by property management. Contact them for specific payment account details and accepted methods.

**Q: What if I notice an error on my invoice?**
A: Contact property management immediately to report billing discrepancies. Provide specific details about the error.

**Q: When is rent typically due?**
A: Due dates are shown on each invoice. Common practice is monthly payment, but check your specific lease agreement and invoice due dates.

### Maintenance Questions

**Q: How do I submit a maintenance request?**
A: Go to Maintenance, click "New Request", select your property, choose category and priority, describe the issue, optionally add photos, and submit.

**Q: What is considered an emergency?**
A: Emergencies include no water/power, gas leaks, severe flooding, broken locks/security issues, or fire safety equipment failure. Use "Emergency" priority for these.

**Q: How can I track my maintenance request?**
A: All requests are listed in the Maintenance section with their ticket number, status, priority, and property details. Click "View" to see full details.

**Q: What are the different maintenance categories?**
A: Categories include Plumbing, Electrical, HVAC, Appliance, Structural, Pest Control, Security, Carpentry, Painting, and Other.

**Q: Can I add photos to my maintenance request?**
A: Yes, you can upload photos or videos when submitting a request. This helps technicians understand and prepare for the issue.

### Technical Questions

**Q: I can't access the portal. What should I do?**
A: Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page to reset your password. If issues persist, contact support.

**Q: How do I update my profile information?**
A: Navigate to Settings > Profile, make your changes, and save.

**Q: Can I access the portal on my mobile device?**
A: Yes, the portal is responsive and works on mobile browsers. Access it through your phone or tablet's web browser.

**Q: I'm not receiving notification emails. Why?**
A: Check your spam/junk folder, verify your email address in Settings, and ensure notification preferences are enabled.

### Account Questions

**Q: How do I change my password?**
A: Go to Settings > Profile or use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page to set a new password.

**Q: Can I have multiple profiles (Tenant, Supplier, Customer)?**
A: If your account has multiple profiles, you can switch between them using the avatar dropdown menu in the top right corner.

**Q: How do I update my contact information?**
A: Go to Settings > Profile, update your phone number, email, or other contact details, and save your changes.
