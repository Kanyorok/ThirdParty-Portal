## Prequalification

### What is Prequalification?
Prequalification is the process of verifying your company's:
- Legal compliance and registration
- Financial stability
- Technical capabilities
- Quality certifications
- Safety standards
- Past performance

### Starting Prequalification
1. Navigate to **Dashboard > Prequalification**
2. Review available prequalification categories
3. Select categories relevant to your business
4. Click "Apply" to start the process

### Prequalification Categories
Common categories include:
- **Construction & Works**: Building, civil engineering, roads
- **Goods & Supplies**: Equipment, materials, consumables
- **Services**: Consulting, maintenance, professional services
- **IT & Technology**: Software, hardware, IT services
- **Specialized Categories**: Industry-specific qualifications

### Required Documentation
Typically required documents:
- **Company Registration**: Certificate of incorporation
- **Tax Compliance**: Tax clearance certificates
- **Financial Statements**: Audited accounts (last 2-3 years)
- **Insurance**: Professional indemnity, liability insurance
- **Certifications**: ISO, quality management certifications
- **References**: Client references and past project records
- **Health & Safety**: Safety policies and records

### Submitting Your Application

#### Step 1: Document Preparation
- Ensure all documents are current and valid
- Scan documents in high quality (PDF preferred)
- File size: Maximum 10MB per document
- Naming convention: Use descriptive names

#### Step 2: Upload Documents
1. Click on each document requirement
2. Click "Upload" or drag-and-drop files
3. Add document expiry dates if applicable
4. Provide any required notes or descriptions

#### Step 3: Additional Information
- Fill in company financials
- Add key personnel details
- List major equipment and assets
- Provide banking information

#### Step 4: Review and Submit
1. Review all sections for completeness
2. Check for errors or missing information
3. Click "Submit for Review"
4. Receive confirmation email

### Tracking Prequalification Status
Monitor your application progress:
- **Draft**: Application in progress, not yet submitted
- **Submitted**: Under review by evaluation team
- **Additional Info Required**: Evaluators need more information
- **Under Evaluation**: Being scored against criteria
- **Approved**: Successfully prequalified
- **Rejected**: Did not meet requirements
- **Expired**: Prequalification period ended

### Responding to Queries
If additional information is requested:
1. Check notifications for query details
2. Navigate to Prequalification > My Applications
3. Click on the specific application
4. Review the query/clarification request
5. Upload additional documents or provide answers
6. Submit response within the given timeframe

### Prequalification Validity
- Most prequalifications are valid for 1-3 years
- Renewal reminders sent 30 days before expiry
- Update documents as they expire
- Maintain compliance throughout validity period

### Prequalification Scoring
Understanding how you're evaluated:
- **Financial Capacity**: 20-30%
- **Technical Capability**: 25-35%
- **Past Experience**: 20-25%
- **Compliance & Certification**: 15-20%
- **Other Criteria**: Varies by category

### Tips for Successful Prequalification
- Start early - process can take 2-4 weeks
- Ensure all documents are valid and current
- Provide clear, complete information
- Respond promptly to queries
- Keep copies of all submissions
- Monitor expiry dates of certificates

### Preparing Your Tender Bid

#### Before You Start
- ✓ Verify you meet eligibility requirements
- ✓ Confirm prequalification status (if required)
- ✓ Read all tender documents thoroughly
- ✓ Understand evaluation criteria
- ✓ Note all deadlines
- ✓ Prepare required documents

#### Tender Bid Components
1. **Technical Proposal**

   - Understanding of requirements

   - Work plan and timeline

1. **Start Application**

2. **Company Information**

   - Verify accuracy

3. **Technical Submission**

   - Add supporting documents
   - Fill in methodology details

4. **Financial Submission**
   - Upload completed BOQ

   - Specify payment terms
   - Add financial notes if needed

5. **Supporting Documents**
   - Upload all required certificates
   - Add references
   - Attach team CVs
   - Include any additional documents

6. **Declaration**
   - Read terms and conditions
   - Check declaration boxes
   - Sign electronically (if available)

7. **Review and Submit**
   - Review all sections
   - Check for completeness
   - Download your bid copy
   - Click "Submit"
   - Receive confirmation email

#### After Submission
- Download submission receipt
- Note your bid reference number
- Monitor for evaluation updates
- Check for any clarification requests
- Wait for evaluation results

### Tender Evaluation Process

#### Evaluation Stages
1. **Preliminary Screening**: Check eligibility and completeness
2. **Technical Evaluation**: Score technical proposal
3. **Financial Evaluation**: Review pricing
4. **Combined Evaluation**: Calculate final scores
5. **Award Decision**: Select winning bidder

#### Evaluation Criteria
Typical scoring:
- **Technical Score**: 70-80%
  - Methodology: 20-25%
  - Experience: 20-25%
  - Team qualifications: 15-20%
  - Understanding: 10-15%
- **Financial Score**: 20-30%
  - Based on price competitiveness

#### Minimum Qualification
- Usually need minimum technical score (e.g., 70%)
- Only then are financial scores calculated
- Lowest price doesn't always win

### After Evaluation

#### If Shortlisted
- Receive notification
- May be invited for presentation
- Provide additional information if requested
- Wait for final award decision

#### If Awarded
1. Receive award notification
2. Contract negotiation begins
3. Sign contract agreement
4. Commence work as per timeline

#### If Unsuccessful
- Receive regret notification
- May request feedback (if policy allows)
- Review for improvement areas
- Continue pursuing other opportunities

### Tender Types Explained

**Open Tenders**
- Open to all qualified suppliers
- Publicly advertised
- Competitive bidding

**Restricted Tenders**
- Only invited/prequalified suppliers
- Limited participation
- Higher value or specialized work

**Request for Proposals (RFP)**
- Emphasis on technical approach
- Quality over price focus
- Complex or consultant services

**Two-Stage Tendering**
- Stage 1: Technical qualification
- Stage 2: Financial bidding
- Used for complex projects

## Request for Quotations (RFQs)

### What is an RFQ?
Request for Quotation (RFQ) is a procurement method for:
- Standard products/services
- Lower value purchases
- Quick turnaround needed
- Well-defined specifications

### Finding RFQs
Navigate to **Dashboard > Find RFQs**

### RFQ Listing
View all available RFQs with:
- **Description**: What's being requested
- **Reference Number**: RFQ identifier
- **Quantity**: Amount needed
- **Deadline**: Quote submission deadline
- **Delivery Required**: When items are needed
- **Status**: Open or Closed

### Viewing RFQ Details
Click any RFQ to see:
- **Detailed Specifications**: Exact requirements
- **Quantities**: Amount of each item
- **Standards**: Quality or compliance requirements
- **Delivery Location**: Where to deliver
- **Delivery Date**: When needed
- **Payment Terms**: Standard payment terms
- **Incoterms**: Delivery terms (if applicable)

### Responding to RFQs

#### Preparing Your Quotation
1. **Review Requirements**
   - Read specifications carefully
   - Ensure you can meet requirements
   - Check delivery timeline feasibility
   - Verify you have items in stock/can source

2. **Calculate Pricing**
   - Unit prices
   - Total prices
   - Discounts (if applicable)
   - Delivery/shipping costs
   - Taxes and duties

3. **Prepare Documents**
   - Product datasheets
   - Certificates of compliance
   - Warranty information
   - Sample images (if requested)

#### Submitting Your Quote

**Step 1: Start Quote**
- Click "Submit Quote" button
- Quote form opens

**Step 2: Pricing Information**
- Enter unit price for each item
- System calculates totals
- Add any applicable discounts
- Include delivery charges if any

**Step 3: Terms and Conditions**
- **Validity Period**: How long quote is valid (usually 30-90 days)
- **Delivery Lead Time**: Days/weeks to deliver
- **Payment Terms**: E.g., Net 30, Cash on Delivery
- **Warranty**: Warranty period offered
- **Return Policy**: If applicable

**Step 4: Supporting Documents**
- Upload product specifications
- Add compliance certificates
- Include product images
- Attach warranty documentation

**Step 5: Additional Information**
- Delivery notes
- Special conditions
- Bulk quantity discounts
- Alternative products (if allowed)

**Step 6: Submit**
- Review all information
- Ensure accuracy
- Click "Submit Quote"
- Receive confirmation

### RFQ Evaluation
Quotes typically evaluated on:
- **Price**: 60-70% weight
- **Delivery Time**: 10-15%
- **Quality/Compliance**: 10-15%
- **Warranty/After-sales**: 5-10%
- **Payment Terms**: 5%

### Quote Status Tracking
Monitor your quotes in My Applications > RFQs:
- **Submitted**: Quote received
- **Under Review**: Being evaluated
- **Clarification Needed**: Buyer has questions
- **Shortlisted**: Among top quotes
- **Accepted**: Your quote was selected
- **Order Placed**: Purchase order issued
- **Not Successful**: Quote not selected

### Responding to Clarifications
If buyer requests more information:
1. Check notification
2. Go to the specific RFQ
3. Review clarification request
4. Provide requested information
5. Upload additional documents if needed
6. Submit response quickly

### After Quote Acceptance
Once your quote is accepted:
1. Receive purchase order (PO)
2. Confirm order receipt
3. Prepare items for delivery
4. Arrange logistics
5. Deliver as per timeline
6. Provide delivery note
7. Submit invoice for payment

### RFQ Best Practices
- **Respond Quickly**: Early submissions show reliability
- **Be Competitive**: Research market prices
- **Be Realistic**: Don't over-promise on delivery
- **Quality Matters**: Don't compromise on specifications
- **Clear Communication**: Be precise in your offer
- **Professional Documentation**: Well-presented quotes
- **Follow Up**: Check status regularly

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General Questions

**Q: Do I need to be prequalified to bid on all tenders?**
A: Not all tenders require prequalification, but many high-value tenders do. Check individual tender requirements.

**Q: How long does prequalification take?**
A: Typically 2-4 weeks, depending on the complexity and completeness of your submission.

**Q: Can I apply for multiple prequalification categories?**
A: Yes, you can apply for as many relevant categories as your business qualifies for.

**Q: How do I know if new tenders are published?**
A: Enable email notifications in Settings, and check the Find Tenders section regularly.

**Q: Can I modify my bid after submission?**
A: Generally no, unless the tender allows amendments. Submit carefully.

**Q: What if I miss the deadline?**
A: Late submissions are typically not accepted unless system issues can be proven.

### Technical Questions

**Q: What browsers are supported?**
A: Latest versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge work best.

**Q: My document upload is failing. What should I do?**
A: Check file size (max 10MB), format (PDF preferred), and internet connection. Try a different browser if issues persist.

**Q: Can I save my application as draft?**
A: Yes, use the "Save Draft" button to save progress and continue later.

**Q: How do I download my submitted bid?**
A: Go to My Applications, find the relevant bid, and click download icon.

### Process Questions

**Q: When will I know tender results?**
A: Timeline varies; typically 30-90 days after tender closing. Check tender details for specific timelines.

**Q: Can I withdraw my bid?**
A: Yes, usually before evaluation starts. Contact procurement team through support tickets.

**Q: What happens after I'm awarded a tender?**
A: You'll receive an award letter and contract for signing. Contract negotiations may follow.

**Q: Do I get feedback if unsuccessful?**
A: Policies vary. Some organizations provide feedback upon request after a waiting period.

**Q: Can I partner with other suppliers for a tender?**
A: If allowed in the tender terms, yes. Joint ventures or subcontracting may be permitted.

### Account Questions

**Q: I've forgotten my password. What do I do?**
A: Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page to reset your password.

**Q: How do I update my company information?**
A: Go to Settings > Profile and update your details there.

**Q: Can I have multiple users from my company?**
A: Contact support to set up multiple user accounts under your company profile.

**Q: How do I close my account?**
A: Contact support with your request. Note that you should complete any ongoing applications first.
