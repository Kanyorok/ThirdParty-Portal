## Submitting Property Interests

### What is a Property Interest?
An expression of interest is a formal application to rent a specific property.

### When to Submit Interest
Submit interest when:
- You've viewed the property and want to proceed
- You've reviewed all details and terms
- You're ready to provide required documentation
- You meet the property requirements

### Before Submitting
Ensure you have:
- ✓ Viewed the property (physically or virtually)
- ✓ Read all lease terms and conditions
- ✓ Prepared required documents
- ✓ Verified you can afford rent + deposits
- ✓ Checked lease start date works for you
- ✓ Understood all fees and charges

### Required Documents
Typically required:
- **Identification**: Passport, national ID, driver's license
- **Proof of Income**:
  - Employment letter
  - Recent pay slips (last 3 months)
  - Bank statements (last 3-6 months)
  - Tax returns (if self-employed)

- **References**:
  - Previous landlord reference
  - Employer reference
  - Character/personal references

- **Other Documents**:
  - Credit report (if available)
  - Proof of address
  - Emergency contact information

### Submitting Your Interest

#### Step 1: Start Application
- Go to the property detail page
- Click "Express Interest" or "Apply Now"
- Application form opens

#### Step 2: Personal Information
Auto-filled from your profile:
- Full name
- Contact details
- Current address
- Email and phone

Verify and update if needed.

#### Step 3: Employment Information
- Employer name and address
- Position/job title
- Duration of employment
- Monthly income
- Employer contact

#### Step 4: References
Provide at least 2-3 references:
- **Previous Landlord**:
  - Name and contact
  - Property address
  - Rental period
  - Reason for leaving

- **Employer Reference**:
  - Contact person
  - Position
  - Contact details

- **Personal Reference**:
  - Name and relationship
  - Contact information
  - How long known

#### Step 5: Additional Information
- **Intended Move-in Date**: When you want to start lease
- **Lease Duration**: How long you want to rent
- **Number of Occupants**: Who will live there
- **Pets**: Type, number, size (if allowed)
- **Vehicle Information**: For parking allocation
- **Special Requests**: Any specific needs

#### Step 6: Upload Documents
Upload all required documents:
- ID copies
- Proof of income
- Reference letters
- Any other requested documents

#### Step 7: Review and Submit
- Review all information for accuracy
- Read terms and conditions
- Check declaration boxes
- Sign electronically (if available)
- Click "Submit Interest"
- Receive confirmation email

### After Submission
- Application goes to property manager
- Typically reviewed within 3-5 business days
- You may be contacted for additional information
- Background and credit checks may be conducted
- References will be contacted

### Interest Status Tracking
Track your applications in **My Interests**:

**Status Indicators**:
- **Submitted**: Application received, pending review
- **Under Review**: Being evaluated by property team
- **Additional Info Required**: Need more documents/details
- **Shortlisted**: Passed initial screening
- **Approved**: Interest accepted, ready for lease
- **Rejected**: Application not successful
- **Withdrawn**: You cancelled the application
- **Expired**: Did not proceed within timeframe

### Responding to Requests
If additional information is needed:
1. Check notification for details
2. Go to My Interests
3. Click on the specific application
4. Review what's needed
5. Upload documents or provide answers
6. Submit response within deadline

### Multiple Applications
You can:
- Apply for multiple properties simultaneously
- Withdraw applications at any time
- Update pending applications
- Each application is evaluated independently

#### Lease Renewal

**Renewal Timeline**:
- Renewal notice: 90 days before expiry
- Respond deadline: Usually 60 days before expiry
- New terms provided: 75 days before expiry
- Decision needed: 60 days before expiry

**Renewal Process**:
1. Receive renewal offer notification
2. Review new terms (rent, duration, changes)
3. Navigate to Leases > Renewal
4. Choose option:
   - Accept renewal
   - Negotiate terms
   - Decline renewal
5. Submit your decision
6. Sign new lease agreement (if renewing)

**Renewal Options**:
- **Fixed Term Renewal**: New fixed period (e.g., 1 year)
- **Month-to-Month**: Convert to monthly tenancy
- **Extended Term**: Longer lease (e.g., 2-3 years)
- **Terminate**: End lease on expiry date

#### Early Lease Termination

If you need to end your lease early:
1. Review lease terms for termination clause
2. Navigate to lease details
3. Click "Request Termination"
4. Select reason for early termination
5. Provide required notice (usually 30-60 days)
6. Submit termination request
7. Property manager reviews
8. Penalties/fees calculated if applicable
9. Move-out inspection scheduled
10. Final settlement processed

**Common Early Termination Reasons**:
- Job relocation
- Financial hardship
- Property issues
- Family circumstances
- Purchasing own property

**Potential Costs**:
- Remaining rent (or portion)
- Re-letting fees
- Break lease penalty
- Loss of deposit (partial/full)

### Move-In Process

After lease approval:
1. **Sign Lease Agreement**: Review and sign digitally or in person
2. **Pay Initial Charges**:
   - First month's rent
   - Security deposit
   - Service charge (if applicable)
   - Other fees

3. **Schedule Move-In Inspection**:
   - Conducted before you move in
   - Document property condition
   - Note any existing damage
   - Take photos/videos
   - Sign move-in checklist

4. **Receive Keys**:
   - Collect property keys
   - Access cards/fobs
   - Mailbox keys
   - Parking remote/card
   - Gate codes

5. **Set Up Utilities** (if not included):
   - Electricity
   - Water
   - Gas
   - Internet
   - Cable/TV

6. **Move In**:
   - Follow building move-in rules
   - Reserve elevator (if required)
   - Introduce yourself to neighbors
   - Familiarize yourself with property

### Move-Out Process

When your lease ends:

**60 Days Before**:
- Confirm move-out date
- Give proper notice if required
- Decide on renewal or termination

**30 Days Before**:
- Schedule move-out inspection
- Start packing
- Arrange movers
- Update address with services

**2 Weeks Before**:
- Clean the property thoroughly
- Repair minor damages
- Remove all belongings
- Dispose of unwanted items properly

**Move-Out Day**:
- Final cleaning
- Return all keys and access cards
- Turn off/transfer utilities
- Meet for final inspection
- Complete move-out checklist
- Hand over property

**After Move-Out**:
- Deposit refund processed (usually 30 days)
- Receive final account statement
- Any deductions explained
- Outstanding charges settled

### Deposit Refund
Security deposit refund:
- Processed within 30-45 days after move-out
- Deductions for:
  - Unpaid rent
  - Property damage beyond normal wear
  - Cleaning (if left unclean)
  - Lost keys/cards
  - Outstanding utilities

- You receive:
  - Detailed statement of deductions
  - Photos of any damage
  - Remaining deposit amount
  - Payment via bank transfer or check

## Maintenance Requests

### When to Submit Maintenance Request
Submit requests for:
- **Plumbing Issues**: Leaks, clogs, no water
- **Electrical Problems**: Power outages, socket issues, lighting
- **HVAC**: Heating/cooling not working
- **Appliances**: Broken fridge, stove, washer
- **Structural Issues**: Cracks, doors, windows
- **Pest Control**: Insects, rodents
- **Security**: Locks, doors, windows
- **Common Areas**: Elevator, hallways, parking

### Emergency vs. Non-Emergency

#### Emergency Requests (Immediate Response)
- No water or electricity
- Gas leak
- Severe flooding/water leak
- No heating in winter
- Broken locks/security breach
- Fire safety equipment failure

**Emergency Procedure**:
1. Call emergency number (provided in lease)
2. Also submit online request
3. Take immediate safety measures
4. Wait for emergency team

#### Non-Emergency Requests (Regular Service)
- Minor leaks
- Worn fixtures
- Paint touch-ups
- Light bulb replacement
- General wear and tear
- Cosmetic issues

**Regular Procedure**:1. Submit online request
2. Expect response within 24 hours
3. Scheduled within 3-5 business days

### Submitting Maintenance Request

Access via **Dashboard > Maintenance**

#### Step 1: Create Request
Click "New Maintenance Request"

#### Step 2: Request Details
- **Category**: Select issue type
  - Plumbing

### Your Responsibilities

#### Tenant Maintenance Duties
You're typically responsible for:
- **Light bulbs**: Interior lighting
- **Filters**: HVAC filter changes (monthly)
- **Batteries**: Smoke detector batteries
- **Cleanliness**: General cleaning
- **Minor Items**: Shower curtains, toilet seats
- **Keys**: Lost key replacement
- **Damage**: Repairs for damage you caused

#### Preventive Maintenance
Help prevent issues:
- Don't flush inappropriate items
- Report small issues before they worsen
- Clean drains regularly
- Change HVAC filters monthly
- Let faucets drip in freezing weather
- Test smoke detectors monthly
- Keep vents unblocked

#### Vendor Access
When technician visits:
- Be home if possible (or grant access)
- Secure pets
- Clear work area
- Describe issue
- Ask questions
- Test repair with technician
- Sign completion form

### Maintenance Best Practices

**Reporting**:
- Report issues promptly
- Be detailed in descriptions
- Add photos for clarity
- Be honest about cause
- Don't attempt complex repairs yourself

**Communication**:
- Respond to messages quickly
- Confirm appointments
- Notify if you need to reschedule
- Be reachable on service day
- Provide feedback after service

**Preparation**:
- Clear access to problem area
- Secure valuable items
- Put pets in safe area
- Be present or arrange access
- Have questions ready

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General Questions

**Q: How do I know if a property is still available?**
A: Properties show real-time availability status. If you can click "Express Interest," it's available.

**Q: Can I view a property before applying?**
A: Yes, always recommended. Schedule a viewing through the property detail page.

**Q: How long does the application process take?**
A: Typically 3-5 business days for review and decision.

**Q: Can I have roommates?**
A: Depends on property policy. Check lease terms and discuss with property management.

**Q: Are pets allowed?**
A: Varies by property. Check individual property pet policy.

### Lease Questions

**Q: Can I break my lease early?**
A: Possible but usually involves penalties. Review your lease early termination clause.

**Q: What happens if I don't renew my lease?**
A: You must vacate by lease end date. Give proper notice per your lease terms.

**Q: Can I sublet my apartment?**
A: Only if permitted in your lease. Most leases prohibit subletting without permission.

**Q: How much notice do I need to give before moving out?**
A: Typically 30-60 days. Check your specific lease terms.

### Payment Questions

**Q: When is rent due?**
A: Usually the 1st of each month. Check your specific lease agreement.

**Q: What payment methods are accepted?**
A: Bank transfer, credit/debit cards, and other methods shown in your payment options.

**Q: What if I can't pay rent on time?**
A: Contact property management immediately to discuss payment arrangements.

**Q: How do I get a receipt for my payment?**
A: Receipts are automatically generated and emailed. You can also download from the portal.

### Maintenance Questions

**Q: What is considered an emergency?**
A: No water, no power, gas leaks, severe flooding, broken locks, no heat in winter.

**Q: How quickly will maintenance respond?**
A: Emergencies: immediately. Urgent: within 24 hours. Normal: 3-5 business days.

**Q: Do I need to be home during maintenance?**
A: Preferred but not always required. You can grant access permission.

**Q: Can I do repairs myself?**
A: Only minor things like light bulbs. Major repairs must be done by property management.

**Q: Who pays for maintenance?**
A: Landlord pays for normal wear and tear. Tenant pays for damage they caused.

### Move-In/Move-Out Questions

**Q: When do I get my keys?**
A: After signing the lease and paying all initial fees.

**Q: What should I do during the move-in inspection?**
A: Document all existing damage, take photos, note everything on the checklist.

**Q: How do I get my deposit back?**
A: Leave property in good condition, clean, all items removed. Refund processed in 30-45 days.

**Q: Can deposit cover last month's rent?**
A: No, deposit is for damages only. Last month's rent must be paid separately.

### Technical Questions

**Q: I can't log in. What should I do?**
A: Use "Forgot Password" link. If still having issues, contact support.

**Q: How do I update my contact information?**
A: Go to Settings > Profile and update your details.

**Q: I'm not receiving email notifications. Why?**
A: Check spam folder, verify email address in settings, check notification preferences.

**Q: Can I access the portal on my phone?**
A: Yes, the portal is mobile-responsive and works on all devices.
